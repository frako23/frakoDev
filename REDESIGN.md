# Redesign Notes

## Direction

The portfolio was moved toward a technical-editorial aesthetic: a dark-first interface with a warm light toggle, stronger typographic hierarchy, and a more deliberate layout. The goal was to make the page feel like a senior engineer's portfolio rather than a generic template.

## What Changed

- Typography: replaced mixed, inconsistent font usage with an intentional pairing of `Fraunces` for display and `Inter Tight` plus `IBM Plex Mono` for body and utility text.
- Colors: replaced the original green-led dark theme with a richer token system using slate backgrounds, a mint accent, a warm secondary accent, and a light theme with cream and charcoal contrast.
- Layout: reshaped the hero into a more editorial two-column composition, softened the portfolio cards into a stronger grid, and simplified resume/contact spacing to feel less template-driven.
- Motion: kept motion purposeful and restrained, mostly hover lift and image scaling, plus the existing typewriter effect in the hero.
- Backgrounds: added layered gradients and a subtle noise/grid atmosphere to give the page depth without visual clutter.
- Theme: added a dark/light toggle with dark mode as the default.

## Why

- The previous version had a generic dark-portfolio look, with repeated green accents and standard card layouts.
- Mixed font families made the system feel accidental.
- Sections lacked a consistent hierarchy, so the site read as a collection of components rather than one designed experience.
- The new structure gives recruiters a faster scan path and a more senior, systems-oriented signal.

## Impact

- Better visual identity and stronger memorability.
- Clearer reading order and stronger CTA hierarchy.
- More cohesive dark mode and a usable light mode.
- Improved perception of polish, maturity, and design intent.
